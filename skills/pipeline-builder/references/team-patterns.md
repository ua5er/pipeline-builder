# How human teams run common goals

Use the closest pattern, or merge two. Keep every review point: each one becomes a gate. "Lead" means the main thread.

## Website or site generator
Team: product lead, UX writer, designer, front-end dev, SEO, QA, client.
1. Brief and positioning (lead), with the audience and one primary action per page. Review: client signs the brief.
2. Information architecture and page list (UX + SEO): keywords per page, internal links. Review: SEO lead.
3. Copy per page (writer) from the facts file. Review: editor for clarity, claims, voice.
4. Visual direction: two directions on one page (designer). Review: client picks one.
5. Build (dev) on the chosen direction, component by component. Review: design QA against the direction.
6. Technical QA: speed, accessibility, schema, links, mobile. Script gates.
7. User test: 5 people try the main task. Review: lead decides fixes.
8. Launch, then analytics on the primary action.
For a generator (many sites from data): add a template stage and a sample stage. Build 1 to 3 samples, gate them hard, then batch with script gates and a sampled critic (1 in 10).

## Video ad (one ad)
Team: strategist, creative director, scriptwriter, storyboard artist, producer, editor, motion designer, sound, QA, media buyer.
1. Research: competitor ads and audience language. Review: strategist.
2. Concept and hooks: 3 to 5 concepts, one line each. Review: creative director picks 1 or 2.
3. Script with shot list and on-screen text. Review: CD plus legal or claims check.
4. Storyboard or animatic. Review: CD; pre-test with viewers (panel) for first 3 seconds.
5. Asset production: shoot, AI generation, stock, voice. Review: producer on technical quality.
6. Edit, motion, sound, captions, safe zones per placement. Review: CD final cut.
7. Platform QA: specs, length, captions, policy. Script gate.
8. Launch as a test; judge by thumb-stop rate, hold rate, CTR, CPA.

## Static ads and banners (batch)
Strategist, copywriter, designer, QA, media buyer.
1. Angles from research. 2. Copy variants. Review: CD. 3. Layout system on one master size. Review: CD. 4. Variants and resizes (script). 5. Panel on concepts (clarity, "what is it", "what do I gain"). 6. Specs QA (script). 7. Launch as a test.

## Landing page
Same as website for one page, plus: offer and proof first, one CTA, message match with the ad, a 5-second test (panel) before launch, A/B plan after.

## SEO article or article series
Team: SEO strategist, writer, subject expert, editor, fact-checker.
1. Keyword and intent research, SERP review. Review: strategist.
2. Brief per article: intent, outline, sources, internal links.
3. Draft (writer). 4. Expert pass for accuracy. 5. Editor: clarity, voice, no AI tells. 6. Fact-check against the facts file. 7. On-page SEO and schema (script). 8. Publish, index, then measure impressions and clicks.
Series: pilot 1 to 2 articles through all gates, then batch with a sampled editor.

## Social post or carousel
Strategist, writer, designer, editor.
1. Angle and hook options. Review: lead picks. 2. Copy. Review: editor (voice, no clichés). 3. Design in the brand system, two directions the first time. Review: lead, then the owner sees it before publishing. 4. Export and specs (script). 5. Publish; judge by saves, comments, profile visits.

## App screen, onboarding or paywall
PM, UX, UI, copywriter, dev, QA, growth.
1. Problem and metric. 2. Flow sketch. Review: PM. 3. Copy. 4. UI in the design system. Review: design lead. 5. Panel walk-through with personas (sequence mode). 6. Build. 7. QA on devices. 8. A/B test on the metric.

## Brand identity or logo
Brand strategist, designer, client.
1. Brand brief and references. 2. Many rough directions. Review: client picks 2 to 3. 3. Refinement with construction rules. Review: client. 4. Small-size and one-colour tests (script). 5. Lockups and usage guide.

## Research report or market analysis
Lead analyst, researchers, data analyst, editor.
1. Questions and hypotheses. Review: lead. 2. Source collection (scripts, scrapers). 3. Coding or tagging (cheap model plus confirm pass). 4. Analysis (scripts compute numbers). 5. Write-up. Review: lead plus fact-check. 6. Executive summary.

## Software feature
PM, engineer, reviewer, QA.
1. Spec and acceptance criteria. 2. Design note. Review: senior engineer. 3. Implementation. 4. Tests (script gate). 5. Code review (critic). 6. QA. 7. Release with monitoring. Usually not consumer-dependent at the gate level; the PM metric judges it later.

## Email or lifecycle sequence
Lifecycle lead, copywriter, designer, deliverability.
1. Segment and goal per email. 2. Copy. Review: editor. 3. Design. 4. Spam and render checks (script). 5. Panel on subject lines. 6. Send as a test; judge by open, click, conversion, unsubscribes.
